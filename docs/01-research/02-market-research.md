## Purpose

Determine whether the problem is large enough, and the target segment reachable enough, to justify building VeriGlow-ai.

## Questions to Answer

| # | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | How large is the online beauty market in the initial target geography, and how fast is it growing? | Defines the opportunity |
| M2 | What share of beauty purchases happen on third-party marketplaces vs. brand stores? | Marketplaces are where trust is weakest |
| M3 | Which segment buys beauty online most often and is most exposed to counterfeits? | Focuses the MVP |
| M4 | What regulation governs cosmetics labelling and online sale in the target market? | Shapes what can be verified |
| M5 | Would buyers, brands, or sellers pay for this — and who first? | Informs business model |
