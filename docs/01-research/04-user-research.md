## Research Goal

Validate (or reject) assumptions **A1–A5** from [01 — Problem Discovery](01-problem-discovery.md#6-assumptions) with real online beauty buyers, and run the must-pass tests **G1–G3** of the validation gate in [03 — Competitor Analysis §13.3](03-competitor-analysis.md#133-validation-gate-4-weeks-no-build) (brand data access, a named payer, frequent and costly doubt).

## Method

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| Method | Semi-structured interviews (30–40 min) |
| Participants | 8–12 people who bought beauty products online in the last 3 months |
| Recruitment | Screener survey |
| Recording | Notes; recordings only with explicit consent |
| Synthesis | Affinity mapping → ranked problem list |

## Interview Themes

1. Last online beauty purchase — walk through it step by step.
2. Moments of doubt about authenticity, expiry, or seller.
3. What they did about that doubt (if anything).
4. Experiences with suspected fake or expired products.
5. Willingness to take an extra step before buying.

> Ask about **past behaviour**, not hypothetical future behaviour. "Tell me about the last time…" beats "Would you use…".
