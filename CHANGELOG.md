### Added

- Master project guide v1.1 (`docs/00-project-management/MASTER_PROJECT_GUIDE.md`): 24 phases, five stages, four gates, milestone tracker.
- Market research v2.0 (`docs/01-research/02-market-research.md`) and its design review record.
- Competitor analysis v2.0 (`docs/01-research/03-competitor-analysis.md`) and its red-team review record. Recommends **STOP of the current thesis + a 4-week validation gate**.
- Repository structure for research, product, design, engineering, testing, deployment, and decision records.
- `README.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, `PROJECT_STATUS.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, and MIT `LICENSE`.
- GitHub issue templates (bug report, feature request) and pull request template.
- Problem discovery document (`docs/01-research/01-problem-discovery.md`).
- ADR template (`docs/07-decisions/ADR-template.md`).

### Changed

- `README.md`, `ROADMAP.md` and `PROJECT_STATUS.md` now follow the master guide's 24-phase, four-gate plan and show the pending competitor-analysis decision.
- Phase labels in document scaffolds aligned with the master guide's phase numbering.
